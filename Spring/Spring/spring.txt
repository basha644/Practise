                     ***Tight Coupling***
Tight coupling occurs when components are highly dependent on each other, with strong knowledge of each other's implementation details.
Characteristics of Tight Coupling:

Components have direct references to each other
Changes in one component often require changes in other components
Components are difficult to test in isolation
Reusability is limited
High maintenance cost

                     ***Loose Coupling***
Loose coupling refers to a design where components have minimal knowledge of each other and interact through well-defined interfaces.
Characteristics of Loose Coupling:

Components interact through abstractions (interfaces or abstract classes)
Changes in one component rarely affect others
Components are easily testable in isolation
High reusability
Lower maintenance cost
Facilitates dependency injection
